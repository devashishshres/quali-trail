AUTOMATIC GEMINI FAILOVER
-------------------------
GEMINI_MODEL remains the primary. On a model timeout/temporary unavailability,
extraction tries GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODELS (comma-separated) in order. If unset,
the defaults are gemini-2.5-flash-lite,gemini-2.5-flash. An empty value disables
backups. Names are deduplicated and at most three models total are attempted,
with a 15-second client deadline each. Invalid credentials/input and malformed
output do not trigger model hopping. Exhausted models retain manual review.
SDK retries are disabled so a slow model cannot consume the fallback window.
Client cancellation does not guarantee provider-side cancellation or no charge.
